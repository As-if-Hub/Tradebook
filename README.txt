TRADE BOOK v2 - how to update (about 5 minutes)

Your trades are safe: they live inside your phone, not in GitHub. Version 2
opens your existing trades automatically and keeps your PIN.

BEFORE YOU START
 1. Open the app, tap More, then Backup and export, then "Save encrypted backup".
    Keep that file. It is your safety net.

UPDATE THE FILES ON GITHUB
 2. Unzip tradebook.zip on your phone or computer.
 3. On github.com open your Tradebook repository.
 4. Tap "Add file", then "Upload files".
 5. Select ALL files from the unzipped folder (8 files):
      index.html, companies.js, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest,
      icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png, README.txt
    Files with the same name are replaced. companies.js is new.
 6. Scroll down and tap "Commit changes".

OPEN THE NEW VERSION
 7. Wait about a minute. Close the app completely and open it again, twice if
    the old screen still shows. The lock screen and then a new look will appear.
 8. Enter your PIN. Your trades are still there.

CHECK THAT PRICES WORK
 - More, then Prices, then "Test the price feed".
 - More, then Prices also lists, for each open trade, whether its price was
   found or why not.
 - A trade whose price was not found: tap it, choose Details, then Edit trade,
   and pick the company from the list. That fixes most missing prices.

APPEARANCE
 - More, then Appearance: Calm Dark, Calm Light or follow your phone, and three fonts.
